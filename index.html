Group worksheet

Use short, clear answers. Reasonable answers supported by the case may be accepted.

Classify the incident

Choose one classification from the lecture.

Identify the first response priority

Choose the first step that applies to this case.

Write two facts that should appear in the report

Facts may include what happened, the time, location, person, item, or observed result.

Identify one contributing condition

What condition may have helped the incident happen?

Prevent the incident from happening again

Give one preventive action and one way to check if it works.
